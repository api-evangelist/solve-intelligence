# Solve Intelligence

> AI-powered software that helps patent attorneys draft, analyze, and manage IP work faster and more accurately

Solve Intelligence builds AI-native software for patent attorneys and IP teams, covering patent drafting, claim charting, invalidity analysis, prior art search, and prosecution workflows. The platform is designed to integrate into existing attorney workflows while maintaining data confidentiality and security standards required by law firms and corporate IP departments.

The site serves patent attorneys, IP professionals, law firms, and corporate legal teams evaluating AI tools for patent work. It includes product information, security details, customer case studies, and an extensive blog covering AI in patent practice, regulatory guidance (EPO, USPTO, UPC), and best practices for adopting AI responsibly in IP work.

Key resources include the product overview, security page, and a large blog archive with articles on AI patent drafting, claim charting, invalidity charts, prior art analysis, ethical guidelines, and firm partnership announcements. Legal pages cover terms, privacy, and general legal information.
